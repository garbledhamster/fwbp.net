# Bryan’s merch customizer

Shoppers choose a product and place artwork from Bryan’s design collection on it.

## Language

**Design**:
One piece of artwork from Bryan’s merch collection.

**Print location**:
An area of a product that can hold artwork. A T-shirt or sweatshirt has a large back print location and a small chest print location.

**Primary design**:
The shopper’s first artwork choice. It starts on the back of apparel and can swap locations with the secondary design.

**Secondary design**:
The shopper’s second artwork choice on apparel. It starts on the chest, can swap locations with Primary, and is remembered while a single-print product is selected.

**Customization**:
The chosen product and the artwork assigned to its print locations. Both locations start blank. A shopper can use the same design in both locations.

**Preview cart**:
A list of customizations the shopper has chosen to keep while trying the customizer. An item needs artwork in at least one print location to be added.

**Printful variant**:
The provider’s catalog entry for a specific product, color, and size. The preview cart records its ID with the shopper’s print locations.
