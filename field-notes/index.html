What is jigging?

Lower the lure to the bottom, reel it up a little, then lift and lower the rod tip. Let the lure settle between lifts so a fish has a chance to bite.

Walleye or yellow perch?

Look for a white tip on the lower part of a walleye’s tail and dark vertical bars on a yellow perch.
