# Fishing with Bryan Campaign

The Campaign is an ice-fishing game on a grid of Mille Lacs fishing spots. Players open holes, catch fish, and learn how to choose where to fish next.

## Language

**Fishing spot**

A selectable location on the lake grid. Its depth, habitat, and permit are map information.

**Open hole**

A fishing spot the player has opened for the current fishing day. The player can return to it without using another drilling slot.

**Hole roster**

The fish present at an open hole for the current fishing day. It stays with the hole as the player moves around or reloads, and changes as fish are caught.

**Recognized at this hole**

A species from Your Lake that is present in the hole's current roster.

**Recognized species**

A fish species the player has caught in Campaign or Practice and added to Your Lake. The player can recognize it at a Campaign hole they open in the future.

**Catch record**

A landed Campaign fish saved with the fishing spot where it was caught.

**Past catch marker**

A map marker for a fishing spot with a saved Campaign catch. The species history belongs to Your Lake.

**Drilling allowance**

The number of new holes a player can open in a fishing day. Players start with two and the Drilling skill raises it to three.

**Your Lake**

The player's collection of caught species and the place to study those fish.

**Studied species**

A recognized species the player has studied in Your Lake to earn its lure hint.
