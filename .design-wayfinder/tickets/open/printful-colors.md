# Question

Does the picker make it clear which color and size the shopper is choosing?

## MVC tracer

- **Route:** The local merch customizer.
- **Controller:** Choose a product, color, and size.
- **Model:** A snapshot of U.S. in-stock variants from Printful’s public catalog. Each row carries the Printful variant ID.
- **View:** Printful swatches, valid sizes, approximate product mockups, and the chosen variant in the preview cart.
- **Return:** Changing color updates the mockup and size list. The cart records the actual color and size combination.

## Prototype boundary

Next Level 3600 is the recorded tee model. The Gildan 18000 crewneck, White Glossy Mug, Classic Dad Hat, and Kiss-Cut Stickers are catalog candidates for the other existing product choices. This checks catalog colors and sizes. Confirm exact techniques, print areas, artwork files, and live stock at order time.

## Completion criterion

Verify the offered combinations against the source snapshot and try changing products, switching colors, and adding a configured item. Joe reviews the picker in the prototype.
